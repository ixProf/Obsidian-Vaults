Lecture 6 notes

* it is possible to produce a completely complete and consistent requirements document in practice ? False *In Practice, it is impossible to produce a complete and consistent requirements document'.*
* Requirements Confusion: Functional and Non-Functional Requirements are mixed-up
* *Glossary* defines Technical Terms
* Open-Interview = Various issues ...etc
* Closed-Interview = based on *pre-determined list of questions*
* Actors and Interactions = *Use Cases*
* نحل مشكلة ال stages (بنبدأ نجمع وبعدها بننظم وبعدها بنرتب وبعدها بنعرض او نحدد) ف ببساطة Discover -> Organize -> Prioritization -> specification 
* RE Process (Elicitation-Analysis-Validation-Management)
* Major guideline for writing requirements to ensure they are mandatory *Use the word shall*
* Main Type of Non-Functional (Product, Organizational, External)
* Type of Product Requirements (Usability, Efficiency(Space, Performance) , Dependability, Security)
* Type of Organizational (Environment, Operational, Development)
* Type of External (Regulatory, Ethical, Legislative "Law"(Safety Requirements, Accounting Requirements))
