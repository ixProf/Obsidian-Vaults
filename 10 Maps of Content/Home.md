# 🏠 Vault Home

This is the primary map of the vault. It is the entry point for navigating domains, source material, projects, and research.

> For the operational view, open [[00 System/Dashboard]].

## Working loop

1. Capture in `01 Inbox`.
2. Process the note into `20 Zettelkasten` — a Literature or Permanent Note, or a Reference Note.
3. Link it to its domain MOC and to at least one real related note.
4. Keep the original long-form source note in `30 Sources`; link it in the atomic note's `source` field.
5. File images under `Attachments/<topic>/` and add them to that topic's gallery note.
6. Review [[00 System/Dashboards/Orphan Notes]] and [[00 System/Dashboards/Metadata Gaps]] regularly.
