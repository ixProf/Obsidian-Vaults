# England

### 1. Gates Cambridge Scholarship
- **Timeline:** Application opens September 2026; Deadline is **early December 2026 or early January 2027** for the Fall 2027 intake.
- **Funding:** Fully Funded (University fee, living allowance, flights, visa).
- **English Proficiency Workaround:** You can apply without a valid English test. If selected, Cambridge will issue a "conditional offer." You will then have until Summer 2027 to take the test and meet the language condition before your Fall enrollment.
- **Application Process:** Submit a standard graduate application via the Cambridge portal and check the "Gates Cambridge" funding box.

### 2. AfOx Graduate Scholarship (Oxford, UK)
- **Timeline:** Application opens September 2026; Deadline is **early January 2027** for the Fall 2027 intake.
- **Funding:** Fully Funded (Oxford tuition, living stipend, flights, visa, health surcharge).
- **English Proficiency Workaround:** You can submit your application for graduate study at Oxford without an English language test score. Your application will still be assessed by the academic department normally. If the department makes you an offer, you will simply be required to submit passing scores later to take up your place.
- **Application Process:** Apply for your target Computer Science Master's at Oxford by January. You are automatically considered for AfOx if you receive an academic offer.

### 3. The DeepMind Scholarships
- **Timeline:** Deadlines vary by partner university, generally closing **early June 2027** for the Fall 2027 intake.
- **Funding:** Fully Funded (Tuition, living stipend, equipment grant, travel allowance).
- **English Proficiency Workaround:** Similar to Oxford and Cambridge, participating UK universities allow you to apply and receive conditional admission based on your technical portfolio. You must pass the English test before securing your final visa in Summer 2027.
- **Application Process:** Apply to and secure a conditional offer from a participating university (e.g., UCL). Then, apply internally for the DeepMind grant.

# Germany

### 1. DAAD Study Scholarships (Standard STEM)
- **Timeline:** Deadline is **October/November 2026** for the Fall (Winter) 2027 intake.
- **Funding:** Fully Funded (€934/month stipend, tuition waiver, health insurance, travel allowance).
- **English Proficiency Workaround:** DAAD normally requires an IELTS or TOEFL certificate at the time of application. Since you do not have a valid score, you have two options: rapidly take the test before the October 2026 deadline, or check if your specific German target programs accept an English Medium of Instruction (EMI) certificate from your Egyptian university, which DAAD sometimes accepts in lieu of standardized tests if the university allows it.
- **Application Process:** Apply via the DAAD online portal, submitting your motivation letter and listing your top 3 chosen Master's programs. 

### 2. German Public Universities (Low-Cost Fallback)
- **Timeline:** University portals generally close **May – July 2027** for the Fall 2027 intake.
- **Funding:** €0 tuition in 14 out of 16 states (only a ~€300 semester fee). 
- **English Proficiency Workaround:** Because applications do not close until Summer 2027, you have ample time to study and pass an English proficiency exam during your senior year. 
- **Application Process:** Apply directly to the university or via Uni-Assist. 

# France

### 1. ENS International Selection
- **Timeline:** Application window is **mid-October to December 11, 2026** for the Fall 2027 intake.
- **Funding:** Fully Funded (€1,000/month for 3 years + accommodation access).
- **English Proficiency Workaround:** ENS evaluates your technical merit, transcripts, and research project first. Standardized English tests are rarely a hard barrier at the initial application stage; a university EMI certificate is often sufficient.
- **Application Process:** Submit transcripts, a motivation letter, and a research project online. Shortlisted candidates take written and oral exams in Feb/March 2027.

### 2. France Excellence Eiffel Scholarship
- **Timeline:** University internal deadlines range from **October to early December 2026**. Campus France final deadline is **January 8, 2027** for the Fall 2027 intake.
- **Funding:** Fully Funded (€1,181/month allowance, international return ticket, health insurance).
- **English Proficiency Workaround:** You do not apply to Campus France directly; you apply to the university. Many French universities will accept an EMI letter from your current university to waive the IELTS/TOEFL requirement for admission.
- **Application Process:** Apply to a French university's Master's program and explicitly request the program coordinator nominate you for the Eiffel Scholarship.

### 3. The Rifaa Al-Tahtawi Scholarship
- **Timeline:** Application opens March 2027; deadline in **April/May 2027** for the Fall 2027 intake.
- **Funding:** Fully Funded (Monthly stipend ~€860, tuition waiver, visa waiver).
- **English Proficiency Workaround:** Requires prior university admission. If the university accepted your EMI certificate, the scholarship will too. 
- **Application Process:** Secure admission to a French university independently first. Then apply through the French Institute in Egypt (IFE) portal.

# US

### 1. Fulbright Foreign Student Program
- **Timeline:** The deadline for the 2027-2028 academic year is **May 31, 2026**. 
- **Funding:** Fully Funded (Full tuition, living stipend, airfare, health insurance).
- **English Proficiency Workaround:** To bypass the lack of a TOEFL/IELTS score, Fulbright Egypt accepts alternative, highly accessible tests for your initial application, such as Duolingo or an approved Egyptian university English test.
- **Application Process:** Apply directly through the Binational Fulbright Commission in Egypt. You secure the scholarship before applying to any US universities.

### 2. Graduate Assistantships (RA/TA) - Research Master's
- **Timeline:** Email professors in **Oct-Nov 2026**. Official university applications due **December 2026 – January 2027** for the Fall 2027 intake.
- **Funding:** Fully Funded (Tuition waiver + living stipend).
- **English Proficiency Workaround:** While you can secure a professor's backing without a test score based purely on your coding portfolio, the graduate school will eventually require a test score (usually by Spring 2027) to finalize your formal admission. 

# Canada

### 1. Thesis-Based Master's (MASc / MSc) with Assistantships
- **Timeline:** Email professors in **Oct-Nov 2026**. Official university applications due **December 2026 – February 2027** for Fall 2027.
- **Funding:** Fully Funded (Tuition + CAD 20,000–35,000 annual living stipend).
- **English Proficiency Workaround:** Similar to the US, you can secure a professor's approval for funding using your technical skills first. You will then receive a conditional admission offer and must take the language test before your visa process in mid-2027.

# Australia

### 1. Research Training Program (RTP)
- **Timeline:** Submit Expression of Interest (EOI) around **July 2026**. Official deadline is typically **August 31, 2026** for a 2027 start.
- **Funding:** Fully Funded (Tuition offset, living stipend, overseas student health cover).
- **English Proficiency Workaround:** Australian universities heavily utilize conditional offers. You can secure the RTP scholarship conditionally and provide a valid English test result later in the year.

# Hungary

### 1. Stipendium Hungaricum
- **Timeline:** Opens in November 2026; Deadline is **January 15, 2027** for the Fall 2027 intake.
- **Funding:** Fully Funded (Free tuition, monthly stipend, accommodation contribution, health insurance).
- **English Proficiency Workaround:** The program natively accepts an English Medium of Instruction (EMI) certificate from your Egyptian university as valid proof, completely bypassing the need for an IELTS or TOEFL.

# Spain (EU-Wide)

### 1. Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters (EMJM)
- **Timeline:** Deadlines vary by specific program, typically **December 2026 to February 2027** for Fall 2027.
- **Funding:** Fully Funded (€1,400/month living allowance, full tuition coverage).
- **English Proficiency Workaround:** Policies vary by consortium. Many EMJM programs allow you to submit an EMI certificate from your undergraduate university, while others allow you to upload test scores up until the final selection phase in Spring 2027.

# Italy

### 1. DSU Regional Scholarships (Need-Based)
- **Timeline:** University applications open **Nov 2026 – Feb 2027**. The DSU scholarship portal opens **July – August 2027** for the Fall 2027 intake.
- **Funding:** Fully Funded (Tuition reduced to €0, €7,000–€8,500 annual cash stipend, free daily university meals).
- **English Proficiency Workaround:** Universities like Politecnico di Milano will assess your technical profile and grant conditional admission. You typically have until the summer of 2027 to provide a valid language certificate to finalize enrollment.
