1. Which phase is not available in the software life cycle?
    

- a) Coding
    
- b) Testing
    
- c) Maintenance
    
- **d) Abstraction**
    

> **Answer: d) Abstraction**

---

2. The third phase in the SDLC is:
    

- a) Coding
    
- **b) Design**
    
- c) Requirement Analysis
    
- d) Conception
    

> **Answer: b) Design**

---

3. Technical knowledge refers to the knowledge of the business application being written and what it is supposed to do.
    

> **Answer: True**

---

4. Acceptance testing is done by .......
    

- a) Developers
    
- **b) Customers**
    
- c) Testers
    
- d) All of these
    

> **Answer: b) Customers**

---

5. Unit testing is done by .......
    

- a) Users
    
- b) Customers
    
- **c) Developers**
    
- d) All of these
    

> **Answer: c) Developers**

---

6. The four variables of software development projects are Cost, Time, Features, and Quality.
    

> **Answer: True**

---

7. Which of the following are the three main variables of the "Iron Triangle"?
    

> **Answer: Cost, Time, and Scope (Features)**

---

8. In the resource requirements part of project planning, we need to answer the following questions except:
    

- a) How many computers are needed?
    
- b) What software will be used for development?
    
- c) What development environment do we need?
    
- **d) What process model the project will be using?**
    

> **Answer: d) What process model the project will be using**

---

9. In ....... model there are no formal requirements, no required documentation, no quality assurance or formal testing.
    

- a) The plan-driven
    
- b) Agile development
    
- **c) Code and fix**
    
- d) None of the above
    

> **Answer: c) Code and fix**

---

10. Code and fix model has:
    

- a) One phase
    
- **b) Two phases**
    
- c) Three phases
    
- d) Four phases
    

> **Answer: b) Two phases**

---

11. Code and fix says take a minimal amount of time to understand the problem and then start coding.
    

> **Answer: True**

---

12. In ....... model, you must finish phase N before you continue on to phase N+1.
    

- **a) The waterfall**
    
- b) Incremental development
    
- c) Code and fix
    
- d) None of the above
    

> **Answer: a) The waterfall**

---

13. Waterfall model is not suitable for:
    

- a) Small projects
    
- b) Large projects
    
- **c) Accommodating change**
    
- d) None of these
    

> **Answer: c) Accommodating change**

---

14. Which of the following statements is not correct about the waterfall with feedback model?
    

- a) It recognizes incomplete requirements
    
- b) It allows going back to previous steps
    
- **c) Both (A) and (B) are correct**
    
- d) None of the above
    

> **Answer: c) Both (A) and (B) are correct**

---

15. Which of the following statements is a disadvantage with the waterfall with feedback model?
    

- a) It messes with scheduling
    
- b) It causes unexpected backward movement
    
- **c) Both (A) and (B)**
    

> **Answer: c) Both (A) and (B)**

---

16. The most suitable model for new technology that is not well understood is:
    

- a) Waterfall model
    
- **b) Evolutionary prototyping model**
    
- c) Code and fix model
    
- d) None of these
    

> **Answer: b) Evolutionary prototyping model**

---

17. Evolutionary prototyping model provides:
    

- a) Progress visibility
    
- b) Customer input
    
- c) Requirement prioritization
    
- **d) All the mentioned above**
    

> **Answer: d) All the mentioned above**

---

18. Evolutionary prototyping works best with tight, experienced teams.
    

> **Answer: True**

---

19. Evolutionary prototyping leads to unrealistic expectations because:
    

> **Answer: All of the above**

---

20. Smaller projects and smaller teams follow more agile development processes.
    

> **Answer: True**

---

21. The agile process model is:
    

- a) Less documentation
    
- b) Small teams
    
- c) Handles change
    
- **d) All the mentioned above**
    

> **Answer: d) All the mentioned above**

---

22. Plan-driven models have clearly defined phases and sign-offs.
    

> **Answer: True**

---

23. Agile models have clearly defined phases and strict sign-offs.
    

> **Answer: False**

---

24. In a plan-driven model, the emphasis is on producing full work products.
    

> **Answer: True**

---

25. In agile methodologies, the code is the sole deliverable.
    

> **Answer: True**

---

26. What does Time-boxed mean in Agile terms?
    

- a) Flexible
    
- b) Fast
    
- c) Frequent
    
- **d) Fixed**
    

> **Answer: d) Fixed**

---

27. In XP, "travel light" means to keep only useful models.
    

> **Answer: True**

---

28. In XP, small releases mean frequent releases.
    

> **Answer: True**

---

29. Refactoring means rewriting code to improve it without changing behavior.
    

> **Answer: True**

---

30. XP requires that a customer representative be part of the team.
    

> **Answer: True**

---

31. Teams that are co-located are worse at communicating than distributed teams.
    

> **Answer: False**

---

32. The best way to reduce turnover is to give developers interesting work.
    

> **Answer: True**

---

33. The best way to mitigate turnover is to spread knowledge among the team.
    

> **Answer: True**

---

34. A defect with no workaround is:
    

- a) Fatal defect
    
- **b) Severe defect**
    
- c) Serious defect
    
- d) Annoying defect
    

> **Answer: b) Severe defect**
