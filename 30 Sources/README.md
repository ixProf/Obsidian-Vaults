## Rules

- Keep source filenames unless a rename is explicitly approved and links are repaired.
- Preserve the original hierarchy (course, level, term, subject, session).
- A Literature Note links to its source here; the source does not link to every extracted note.
- Arabic filenames and content are preserved byte-for-byte.
- Source notes may remain long and unpolished. Do not atomise them in place.
