

1. ....... is the process of taking a set of requirements from a user (a problem statement), analyzing them, designing a solution to the problem, and then implementing that solution on a computer.

- a) Programming
    
- **b) Software Development**
    
- c) Software Validation
    
- d) Software Evaluation
    

> **Answer: b) Software Development**

2. ....... involves a process and includes software development, but it also includes the entire management side of creating a computer program that people will use.

- **a) Software engineering**
    
- b) Programming
    
- c) Software Validation
    
- d) Software Evaluation
    

> **Answer: a) Software engineering**

3. Programming is specifically defined as:

- a) The entire management side of a project.
    
- **b) The implementation part, or possibly the design and implementation part, of software development.**
    
- c) The process of gathering requirements from a customer.
    
- d) A simple shared story of how the system works.
    

> **Answer: b) The implementation part, or possibly the design and implementation part, of software development.**

4. According to the SDLC (Software Development Life Cycle), what is the third phase?

- a) Coding
    
- b) Requirement Analysis
    
- **c) Design**
    
- d) Conception
    

> **Answer: c) Design**

5. Software architecture refers to:

- a) The detailed study of the development of the project.
    
- b) The internal details of major components of a system.
    
- **c) The overall structure of the system.**
    
- d) The properties of the project.
    

> **Answer: c) The overall structure of the system.**


6. ....... means spreading the collective knowledge of the group around to all the members.

- a) Simplicity
    
- b) Feedback
    
- **c) Communication**
    
- d) Courage
    

> **Answer: c) Communication**

13. What are the four variables of software development projects?

- **a) Cost, Time, Features, and Quality.**
    
- b) Analysis, Design, Implementation, and Testing.
    
- c) Requirement, People, Tools, and Process.
    
- d) Coding, Testing, Maintenance, and Release.
    

> **Answer: a) Cost, Time, Features, and Quality.**

14. Which of the following is a disadvantage with the waterfall with feedback model?

- a) It is too fast for most teams to handle.
    
- b) It messes with your schedule because in any phase there can be unexpected moves back to a previous phase.
    
- **c) Both (A) and (B) are disadvantages.**
    

> **Answer: c) Both (A) and (B) are disadvantages.**

- (Note: Source 441 confirms both "messes with scheduling" and "harder to know when finished" are disadvantages ).
    

15. ....... is probably the largest single reason for missed delivery dates, high defect rates, and project failure.

- **a) Requirements churn**
